---
name: Connect Sphere content authenticity
description: Project-specific rule against sample product records and simulated product interfaces.
---

Do not seed fictional product, article, release, pricing, or compatibility data. Display real published records and explicitly label unavailable or unverified capabilities. Never present simulated DAW or plugin interfaces as product screenshots.

**Why:** The user explicitly required no demo mocks or simulations; the project must not imply that LaneuCap features or downloads are real without verified evidence.

**How to apply:** Source catalogue, news, learning, release, and account views from actual persisted records; require real assets and files before exposing them; use honest empty states for missing data.
